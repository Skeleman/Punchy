## Zach's notes (verbatim)

> Sea swells have a very discernable repeated pattern. THey are also rolling away from the island. Sea breeze should push waves inward. It would be nice if the waves could crest and break within a certain distance of the shore as well. The displacement looks pretty good for empty sea, however, at low angles, where waves are viewed through each other, the transparency layering doesn't make sense.
> Surf line looks passable, but not ideal. A little smooth and soft, and it feels werid that the waves are pulling away from its direction, but that's more of a problem with the waves.
> Not sure I see whitecaps anywhere.
> The snell's window looks pretty good. Any way to make it sample from the player? Or could it fade into another representation as you get closer to the surface so you can start seeing some detail from above? Also, the transition to the surface is very slow, so the sky remains teal and it looks like the world eventually pops in.
> Not sure how to enter commands, so i cannot test lava or the meadow.
> Blue moon streaks end abrubply. They either need to continue further into the distance or taper into nothing, perhaps turning into sparkles as they fade away
> Gras gusts look good, but none of the other flora react to it. Honestly, the animations for most flora just look like something is inflating and twisting. Island V3 changed the colors of everything relative to V2, but the models didn't, so some of the floor looks a lot greener than the grass now. Is there a way to sync this up better?
> Cumulonimbus cloud looks like a giant sky mushroom. It's just a cylinder until the very top, where it spreads. Not very convincing.
> The lighting of the regular moon and blue moon should be additive. THe blue moon should be a much stronger light source. There should be a significant blue tint to the lighting when it is out.
> When cycling through weather, it would be nice to get a prompt displaying what type of stomr is present
> The sky serpents look jittery from a distance. If the posing is supposed to be updated every frame, they are still not drawn every frame.
> Salt ball shadow does not reflect its geometry. It looks like it updates every ~3 seconds, and it has a flat edge.
