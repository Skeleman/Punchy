## Animal behavior manager

### Bugs
1. **Scared creatures in caves run through walls.** Fleeing must respect cave walls (FloorUnder / CaveDistance), and
   pick escape directions along passages.
2. **Camera in caves can't look up or down.** Instead of blocking rotation, pull the camera radius in when a cave
   wall is in the way (spring-arm style). (`ThirdPersonCamera.cs`)
3. **Default scene still has the original capsule collider with the mask.** Find out why it's there; remove it if
   nothing needs it, or explain. (Scene: no owner, so tell the supervisor what changes.)
4. **Overlay should show x/y/z coordinates** so Zach can report locations. (`DebugControls`) Do this first: it helps
   every other report.

4b. **Folk heads:** eyes sit very low and everyone looks bald; likely a part-alignment issue (eye and hair
    sockets vs head size and offset, race scale overrides).

### Polish
4c. **Hair variety:** some NPCs bald; many hairstyles, varying by race (plus beards and moustaches where they fit).
5. **Dogs:** legs a little long or torso too short; give the face a separate snout piece with a clear nose. (Resolves
   the held dog question.)
6. **Cats:** legs a little long; ears too small.
7. **Chickens:** legs and neck far too skinny.
8. **Human sprint:** longer, slower strides.
9. **Holding an animal:** arms shouldn't fall back to the run swing; hold the animal and match its pose.
10. **Snakes:** the slither reads as tail-wagging. Make it a travelling wave along the body, with the body following
    the head's path (sidewinding or serpentine), not a waving tail.
11. **Creature pop-in** is obvious on empty flat biomes: fade or fizzle creatures in and out (shader dither by
    spawn age, no extra draws).
12. **Footprints on glowing terrain** look odd: match the ground's glow so they blend. *Clarified:* the red-hot
    glowing ground (Ashlands, volcano slopes, lava edges).
13. **Controller bindings** for the new actions (pet, carry, attack, and any emotes).
14. **Bird nests:** a contact shadow or darkening beneath them. (World does the general version; see W-14.)

9b. **Carrying:** not possible in an animal body (petting stays); held animal sits at the carrier's own chest height
    (scaled by species and FolkScale).

### New
15. **NPCs (and some animals) react to the player's emotes.**
16. **Some land animals can swim** (e.g. dogs, deer, bears, horses), crossing rivers and lakes.
17. **Very fast horses in the salt flats** (a herd that gallops, flees fast).
18. **Ghosts at night** in the foggy marsh, the poison swamp, and near ruins and graveyards (J-020). Translucent,
    drifting, fade by daylight. Ruins and graveyards as landmarks are the world session's (W-24).
19. **A bone lizard that lives in lava** (lava pools and volcano craters; skeletal, glowing).

19c. **Play as a bird and fly; more playable animals** (Zach, 2026-10-28). After the carry batch.
19d. **Lizards and spiders climb walls** (cliffs, cave walls and ceilings, trunks, buildings); playable too. Same batch
    as 19c.

## world feature manager

### Bugs
1. **Cave entrances still have open seams:** not continuous with the outside terrain.
2. **Ice cave near the second N press: a geometry face sticks through the ceiling.** Find the source.
3. **Water edges sometimes calculate wrong** (long-standing; more visible with the new shore line).
4. **Floating ground-detail debris** where land was removed for a cave opening, visible looking out of a cave.
5. **Cave streams:** the movement shader is unconvincing, and the water doesn't fill the channel properly.
6. **An accidental-looking cliff** between the salt flats by the ocean, a beachy desert biome and the poison swamp.
   Zach will send coordinates once the overlay shows them. (He likes deliberate cliffs; see W-22.)
7. **Poison swamp geyser has rough edges.**
8. **Crystals on volcano slopes don't get atmospheric fading** like the land, so they stand out at a distance.

### Polish
9. **Ice caves:** a little more wall geometry; some slick, flat, glassy wall regions; ice sheets on the powder-snow
   floor here and there (melted and refrozen). Zach loves the current textures and wants variation.
10. **Cracks in ice and lava caves** like the metal and crystal ones Zach loves: dark blue or black on ice, glowing
    red or orange in lava.
11. **Lava** looks like molten gold: make it redder with yellow highlights, black hard cooling patches on the surface,
    and a little bubbling.
12. **Lakes:** stiller, shorter, crisper surface detail instead of fast, wide, amorphous waves. The moon reflection is
    excellent; match its detail across the water.
13. **Rain puddles fade in** instead of appearing suddenly.
14. **Cheap ambient occlusion / contact darkening** under objects (rocks, props, plants, nests) so transitions aren't
    jarring. Shared with the animal session for nests.
15. **Overworld textures need more detail** (rocks especially), to match the caves. Terrain, rocks and props.
16. **Rocks on slopes and edges** should tilt to the ground; sinking in a little is fine. (`FloraPlacement`, shared)
17. **Pillars (columns) much more common**, including in small caves.
18. **Glowing mushrooms as a weak light source** that also lights creatures and the player, not only cave rock (if
    it's cheap enough). *Clarified:* the light must fall on the surroundings (ground, walls, plants, creatures),
    in caves and on the surface; a bright glow in darkness looks odd.
18b. **Roots through cave ceilings** in biomes where it makes sense (forests, marsh, fungal and limestone near the
    surface), thickest near mouths and under trees above.
18c. **Drips** from the ceiling, especially from stalactite tips onto stalagmites, with a small splash or ripple.
    Cheap: particles near the player only, seed and clock driven.
19. **The great tree:** raise its canopy so it makes a clearing and lets more light in.

19b. **Grass wind from a scrolling noise texture** (patchy rolling gusts, Roystan-style), on _PunchyClock.
19c. **Denser, more consistent grass:** ground tint under grass, distance falloff, tufts; then 1.5x and 2x density
    variants with screenshots and a cooled A/B so Zach can pick density vs frame time.

19e. **Biome-border plant spill:** very different neighbours shouldn't mix flora (e.g. dead black trees in forests);
    similar ones can blend (Zach, 2026-10-28).
