# v0.15 candidate: what to check

Tick what you try; note anything off with place, time of day and preset. Sections run newest first. Skip a v0.14

## New since v0.14 (not in any release yet)
- [ ] **Play as sea, bat or turtle folk** (character menu). Sea folk: breath lasts while swimming, never drains under
  water; others must resurface. Bat folk: night vision brightens the dark.
- [ ] **Sea folk and turtle folk live in their homes**: the lake village and the mountain igloos are lived in.
- [ ] **Households**: folk walk home at dusk to their own house, and talk about family by name.
- [ ] **Riding**: mount any creature at least half again heavier than you; a player can ride another player, and
  the one ridden keeps control. Riding a colossus, and climbing one.
- [ ] **Climbing**: ladders on docks, roofs and in mines; folk climb ladders and marked walls; ledge hang and shimmy.
- [ ] **Fishing**: fish notice the bobber; catches depend on the water.
- [ ] **Harsh-place work**: hermit chores and talk in harsh biomes.
- [ ] **Shadows**: one shadow darkness at every distance (no darker or lighter bands as you look farther out).
- Anything merged before the build goes in this list (I'll update it when I cut the build).
