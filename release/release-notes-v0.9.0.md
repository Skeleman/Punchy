# Punchy v0.9.0

## New since v0.8.0

### Playing as animals
- **Birds**: the left stick steers flight while the camera stays put; ground animals no longer scatter from a high flyer.
- **Emotes** as an animal play its own call.
- **Petting** with a front paw when your body has no hands.
- **Horses and dogs gallop**: longer strides, fewer steps, a moment in the air.
- **Breath** shows in the cold for warm-blooded animals.

### Creatures
- Feet stay planted when animals start, stop or break into a run.
- Snakes no longer wag or shake their heads at speed.
- Far fewer overlapping bird calls in villages and forests.
- Smoother towns: animals arrive a few per frame instead of all at once, and creature drawing is cheaper.

### Sound in caves
- Surface sounds fade underground, and caves echo by their size.

### World
- **Caves**:
  - streams run unbroken and end in pools;
  - smoother stream bends;
  - no more stray holes or surface rocks in cave openings;
  - cave walls near an opening match the ground outside;
  - mine frames reach the roof.
- **Landmarks**:
  - lakes no longer hang over broken rims;
  - waterfalls pour from the cliff top into their pool, with plants around them;
  - rocks on crests sit in the ground;
  - mesa arches are seated and rarer;
  - crystals hold perfectly still;
  - the poison geyser is easier to read.
- **Towns**:
  - elven towers are wider and taller;
  - elven benches are stone;
  - floor mats sit before some doors;
  - market awnings move gently;
  - signposts stand only at real forks;
  - you can stand on hut roofs.
- Smoother cave streaming, and weather that starts faster.

## Multiplayer
- **Builds**: everyone needs v0.9.0 (v0.8.0 players can't join a v0.9.0 game, or the other way round).
- **Tested** by hosting and joining.

## Download
Unzip `Punchy-v0.9.0-windows.zip` anywhere and run `Punchy.exe`. Windows 64-bit only.
