# v0.17 playtest: what to check

Tick what you try; note anything off with place, time of day and preset. The aim is to judge the visual direction. Earlier list: checklist-v0.16.md.

- [ ] **Rivers**: channels follow the ground; no water walls, uphill water or sheets diving into ground. Steep drops become cascades with pools.
- [ ] **Waterfalls**: curtains with rock shoulders, wet stones and spray; every fall lands in a pool.
- [ ] **Ponds and lakes**: small ponds sit in a bowl; lakes meet rivers at one level; shores blend with a lapping line.
- [ ] **River life**: cattails, reeds, driftwood heaps, logs, gravel islands, stepping stones, cut banks. Too much or too little?
- [ ] **Water surface**: ripples, foam with the current, rock wakes, caustics in sunny shallows, mist at dawn and dusk.
- [ ] **Ground**: grass ground shows blade strokes up close; dirt looks grainy at 20 m; no flat paint.
- [ ] **Rock and caves**: cliffs take their biome's stone; cave mouths have no seams; mushrooms, tendrils, haze.
- [ ] **Bark and wood**: bark differs by species, pine bark plates, wood grain on buildings.
- [ ] **Lava**: crust rafts; lava seeps run down rock as small lava falls.
- [ ] **Falling details**: needles under pines, flakes under giant caps, grit from cave ceilings (subtle on purpose).
- [ ] **Towns**: kit houses, huts, inns, halls; roofs suit the biome (clay in deserts, check a cold town); tile roofs hold their pattern at a distance.
- [ ] **Walk-in places**: elf towers with a roof hatch, walk-in houses, castle ruins with a gatehouse, piers.
- [ ] **Play**: no loading screen; chests open; households react to theft; you start with a torch.
- [ ] **Performance**: frame rate on your usual preset in a river valley and a town.
