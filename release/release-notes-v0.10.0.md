# Punchy v0.10.0

## New since v0.9.0

### Playing as animals
- **Climbing**: monkeys, cats, squirrels, bears, lizards and spiders climb tree trunks (walk into one to take hold;
  jump lets go). Other players see you climb.
- **Mantling**: climbing onto a ledge keeps your hands on the rim while your body rises.
- **Birds** fly faster, each at its own speed. The left stick also eases you up and down; jump and dive change
  height more boldly, and a dive picks up speed like a real one. Birds land on perches and slide off trees instead
  of flying through them.
- **Fish** hop along on land, follow the camera up and down, and leap out of the water from near the surface.
- The bone lizard walks on land.
- **Emotes start conversations**: folk who answer your wave, bow, cheer or dance say a line back, and animals react
  in their own way (dogs come over wagging, cats come partway and watch, some flee, some ignore you).

### Creatures and folk
- **Folk talk**: villagers say lines as you walk by.
- Folk carry torches or lanterns, by how advanced their people are.
- Woodcutters chop at the block and smiths hammer at the anvil.
- **New creatures**: an ocotillo mimic that folds its arms and scuttles off like a spider, spiky mud-pit pigs in
  the ashlands, and ghosts and skeletal creatures in bone graveyards.
- Oases have frogs and birds; the great tree is full of fireflies in many colours; fish swim in cave ponds.
- Rare gold-coated animals (about 1 in 400).
- Swimmers leave wakes and ripples in the water instead of splashes.
- Softer footsteps that depend on the ground; songbirds perch in the trees, not above them; the crow is black;
  drinking no longer lifts an animal's back legs.

### World
- **Roads** curve and wander, and no longer run into houses.
- **Biomes**:
  - the fungal forest has plain caps that glow only in their gill veins, on drier, paler ground;
  - ember forests glow in their leaves and needles;
  - the electric wastelands have enormous floating magnetic rocks;
  - glaciers have many ice arch shapes;
  - rocks take the colour of the ground they sit on (sandy in deserts, not grey and mossy).
- **Water**: geysers and hot springs bubble; cave creeks have detailed white water; lakes cut by a river no
  longer hang as walls of water.
- Cave mouths blend into the ground outside; the blue moon goes through its phases.
- Smoother streaming on the way into caves.

## Multiplayer
- **Builds**: everyone needs v0.10.0 (v0.9.0 players can't join a v0.10.0 game, or the other way round).
- Joining no longer throws errors as the second player arrives.
- **Tested** by hosting and joining.

## Download
Unzip `Punchy-v0.10.0-windows.zip` anywhere and run `Punchy.exe`. Windows 64-bit only.
