# Punchy v0.16.0

## New since v0.15.0

### World and look
- **Cliffs are built into the ground**: big cliffs, river banks, sea cliffs and mesas now come out of the ground
  itself instead of being stuck on.
- **The sea** rolls with a proper swell.
- **Water**: no stray flat water discs, rain hollows match across distances, and rice paddies no longer spike up or
  hold water on slopes.
- **Cave mouths** keep their first stretch built as you walk in.
- **Landmark gardens** sit on level beds.

### Folk
- **Lamplighters** light each town at dusk.
- **Fires give heat**, and nobody walks through a campfire.
- Folk **read the notice board** and pin sheets up.
- **Household pets**: folk keep animals and pet them in their own way.
- Doors: an open door lets more sound through.
- Far-off town figures fade in and out smoothly.
- Robes, aprons and capes hang clear of the legs.
- Playtest fixes from v0.15.

### Creatures
- **Every animal has its own build and gait**, and coat styles really vary.
- Bodies read more clearly: arms meet the torso, dogs and wolves have proper faces, birds have layered feathers.
- Ladders climb briskly.

## Known issues
- Lying logs sway a little in the wind, and their bark runs round the log instead of along it (#359).
- Waterfall sheets, river course fixes and the shared cliff and cave wall are still being worked on.
- Builds older than v0.16.0 can't join a v0.16.0 game.
