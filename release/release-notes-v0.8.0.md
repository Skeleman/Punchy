# Punchy v0.8.0

## New since v0.7.0

### Five new biomes, and a steadier island
- **Fungal forest**: giant breathing mushrooms with gills, puffballs and glowing bracket fungus.
- **Ember forest**: burnt trees with pulsing ember seams in the bark.
- **Mesa canyonlands**: terraced red canyons with banded rock, and arches and hoodoos that match the cliffs.
- **Glass desert**: glossy sand, teal glass plates, fulgurites and giant bones.
- **Electric wastelands**: obsidian spires that crackle, with bolts jumping between them.
- The island is laid out again, one last time. New landmarks and biomes now only change the spots they win, so future updates won't reshuffle everything.

### Caves, mines and towns
- **Caves**:
  - bigger cave networks;
  - jagged, ribbed-ice, metallic and crystal looks;
  - lots of rubble, boulders made from the cave's own rock;
  - waterfalls, and torches with real flames.
- **Mines**: timbered tunnels, with a dwarf mining camp next to them.
- **Towns**:
  - ring lanes, and houses filling in as if the town grew over time;
  - fire pits at the centre;
  - benches, logs and stumps to sit on;
  - fenced roads leading in.
- **Buildings**:
  - foundations, so nothing floats;
  - porches on round huts and towers;
  - windows set into the walls;
  - matte stone, dirt toward the base and dark eaves;
  - banners that move in the wind.

### Climb, sit and stand on things
- Climb onto rocks, roofs, fountains and wells: jump at a ledge and pull yourself up. Wade in fountain basins.

### People
- **Guards**:
  - patrol set routes in race-specific armour;
  - draw their weapons when there's danger;
  - chase off wolves and bears that wander into town.
- **Everyday life**:
  - fishers by the water, swimmers in the warm middle of the day;
  - miners carrying ore;
  - children playing with sticks and stones, now with child proportions.
- **Evenings**: villagers gather round the fire at dusk, sitting on benches or the ground, cross-legged or lying back. Lanterns come out after dark.
- **Visitors**:
  - travellers walking the roads;
  - messenger birds landing on a villager's arm with a letter.
- **Everyone**: looks around at what's nearby.

### Animals and sound
- **The world has sound**:
  - footsteps, each species' own calls, purring cats and panting dogs;
  - fire crackle, waterfalls, cave drips and town chatter.
- **Small life is real**:
  - songbirds, owls, woodpeckers, frogs and mice;
  - insects and bees round wild hives, with bears raiding them now and then.
- **New**: the winged sky serpent, rising out of deep lakes.
- **Look**: gear shows metal, leather, cloth and wood; eyes you can see; slower, more natural wingbeats.

### Sky
- Two moons: a phased main moon and a small blue moon on its own path.

### Performance and fixes
- Windows players now start on Direct3D 11, which runs noticeably smoother here and fixes a crash on quit.
- Many smaller fixes:
  - no more creature or fence overlaps;
  - fewer butterflies and village animals;
  - creature counts capped sensibly.
- Starts in fullscreen.

## Multiplayer
- **Builds**: v0.7.0 players can't join a v0.8.0 game (or the other way round). Everyone needs v0.8.0.
- **Tested** by hosting and joining over the internet:
  - the same world, clock and moons on both sides;
  - creatures and small animals in agreement.

## Download
Unzip `Punchy-v0.8.0-windows.zip` anywhere and run `Punchy.exe`. Windows 64-bit only.
