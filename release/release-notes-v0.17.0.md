# Punchy v0.17.0

## New since v0.16.0

### Rivers and water
- **Rivers rebuilt**: shaped channels, waterfalls with curtains, rock shoulders and plunge pools, and lakes that meet their rivers at the same level.
- **River life**: cattails, reeds, driftwood heaps, logs, gravel islands, stepping stones, point bars and overhanging cut banks.
- **Water surface**: flow ripples, foam that follows the current, broken foam rims, rock wakes, caustics in sunlit shallows, and mist at dawn and dusk.
- **River beds and banks**: cobbles, sand and silt by flow speed, plus wet bands, mud and moss on rocks.
- **Water stays level**: steep drops become cascades with pools, closed pits fill flat, and small ponds sit in their own bowls.

### Rock, caves and materials
- **One material library** for stone, wood, metal, cloth and creature coats. Cliffs, scarps and caves take their biome's stone.
- **Caves**: no more seams or see-through mouths, real mushrooms, glowing tendrils, roots and ropes, cave haze, and climbable shelves.
- **Bark and wood**: cracked bark by species, pine bark plates, strangler-fig roots, and wood grain with knots and wear.
- **Ground texture**: grassy ground shows blade strokes and bare dirt is speckled and grainy.
- **Falling details**: pine needles drift from conifers, flakes from giant mushroom caps, and grit trickles from cave ceilings.
- **Lava** has crust rafts, and lava seeps run down the rock as small lava falls.

### Places
- **Castle ruins** with a gatehouse.
- **Farm fields** round towns, including rice, kale beds and kelp racks.
- **Houses, huts, inns, halls, tents and stalls** built from one kit, with sloped roof collision. Roofs, plaster and thatch follow the town's biome, and tile roofs keep their pattern at a distance.
- **Walk-in elf towers** with a roof hatch, and **walk-in houses**.
- **Piers** with plank stairs, rope and fish drying racks.

### Play
- No loading screen: the camera holds on the sky while the world builds.
- Cave and ruin chests open, dungeon quests lead to a chest, and dungeon creatures roost, patrol and sleep.
- Households own their houses and goods, and get upset when they see a theft.
- You start with a torch, and the cave lantern needs a light item in hand.
- Smoother movement on long frames, and soft wide shadows.

## Known
- Water seeps are not placed yet; only lava seeps appear.
- The world layout changed. Old saved positions may land somewhere new.
