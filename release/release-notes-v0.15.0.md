# Punchy v0.15.0

## New since v0.14.0

### Player
- **Play as sea folk, bat folk or turtle folk** from the character menu. Sea folk can stay under water as long as
  they keep swimming; everyone else has to come up for air. Bat folk see in the dark.
- **Riding**: climb on any creature at least half again heavier than you. One player can ride another, and the one
  being ridden keeps control. You can ride a colossus, and climb one.
- **Climbing**: ladders on docks, on roofs and in mines. You can hang from a ledge and shimmy along it.
- **Fishing**: fish notice the bobber, and what you catch depends on the water.

### Creatures and folk
- **The sea folk lake village and the turtle folk igloos have people living in them.**
- **Households**: folk go home at dusk to their own house and talk about their family by name.
- Folk climb ladders and marked walls, do hermit chores in harsh places, and reach for a pocket, pouch, coat or
  shoulder when they put an item away or take one out.
- Animals that lie down (horses, deer, cattle) fold their legs under them.

### World and look
- **Shadows** are the same darkness at every distance. The sun moves in small steps so shadows no longer crawl,
  and at High and Ultra a wider filter softens each step. Grass in shadow at middle distance is shaded too.
- **Cliffs and steep slopes** show layered rock with beds and slabs at High and above.
- At High and Ultra the island's far map is sharper (4096).
- Inside caves there is no sunlight or cloud shadow on the walls.

## Known issues
- Lying logs sway a little in the wind, and their bark pattern runs round the log instead of along it (#359).
- Puddles can stop with a hard edge where two patches of ground meet (#357).
- Builds older than v0.15.0 can't join a v0.15.0 game.
