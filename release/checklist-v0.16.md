# v0.16 playtest: what to check

Tick what you try; note anything off with place, time of day and preset. Earlier lists: checklist-v0.15-candidate.md.

- [ ] **Cliffs**: big cliffs, river banks, sea cliffs and mesas look like part of the ground (no seams, floating rock or holes).
- [ ] **Sea swell**: the sea rolls; whitecaps and surf still line up.
- [ ] **Water**: no flat water discs; puddles and hollows don't step at distance; paddies on slopes have no spikes.
- [ ] **Caves**: walking into a cave mouth, the first stretch never pops in or vanishes.
- [ ] **Towns at dusk**: a lamplighter walks round lighting lamps.
- [ ] **Fires**: folk walk round campfires and gather near them when cold.
- [ ] **Notice board**: folk stop to read it and pin sheets.
- [ ] **Pets**: houses with a dog, cat or small animal; folk pet them.
- [ ] **Far towns**: figures fade in smoothly, no popping.
- [ ] **Clothes**: robes, aprons and capes don't cut through legs when walking or sitting.
- [ ] **Animals**: herds show different builds and gaits; coats vary; wolves and dogs have clear faces; birds feathered.
- [ ] **Ladders** feel brisk.
