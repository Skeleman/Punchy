# Punchy v0.14.0

## New since v0.13.0

### Player
- **Talking to folk**: walk up to a villager and press Use to open a talk window. Folk stop, turn to you and
  answer by topic (who they are, how they are, their work, their town, news, a favour). Each has a name and a
  personality of their own, and greets you differently the second time. Some offer a quest or a small gift.
- **As an animal**, folk react in character instead: they pet a rabbit, back away from a wolf, shoo vermin, puzzle
  over a horse and marvel at the biggest beasts.
- **Gear**: torch, staff, hat and pack. Put them on from the inventory (X or right click); the torch gives real
  light and the pack adds 8 slots. Other players see what you wear. Gear turns up in dungeon chests and as quest
  rewards.
- **Items** have their own small models in the world and icons in the inventory and chests.
- **Quests**: the quest log shows progress and where to hand in, and you can follow one quest. New delivery and
  visit quests send you between towns. Quest progress is saved between sessions.
- **Doors**: the door prompt shows reliably as you reach a door, from any angle. Indoors the camera pulls in to suit
  the room, and the camera distance setting now goes all the way down to first person.
- **Stats**: a Stats tab in the menu shows your strength and other stats, which depend on your body.
- The small-body camera behaves in caves; roof undersides are lit like the walls; display modes in the settings.

### Creatures and folk
- **Fur** on furry animals, with a soft outline and smoother joints. Per-part surfaces: fur of different lengths,
  feathers, scales, eyes, hooves and real paws.
- Animals lie down properly on flat ground and slopes, and get up before they move.
- Breath in the cold scales with body size: birds leave one faint puff, mammoths and snow worms a big cloud.
- No more white sparkles on rabbits.
- Creature extras (torch flames, bats, bees, footprints, wake ripples) fade by how big they look on screen, so
  nothing pops at odd distances.

### World
- **Road tunnels** go through mountain ranges and big hills. The longest runs over a kilometre under the border range,
  with a waystation at each end, lanterns and a shrine inside. Roads now reach every mine, some caves and the sea
  folk landing, with loops and the odd ruined bridge.
- **Dungeons** are dressed in stone, wood and iron with real props, and have names. Caves are about twice as big,
  and ruins about four times, with two ruins on the island. Dungeon chests are solid: nothing falls through or sticks.
- **Beacon towers** stand in big town squares, and you can climb them; their fire shows from far away. Towns are lit
  better at night.
- **Inn** rebuilt; fishing boats float at the waterline; cave doorways and cave mouths are walkable and seamless.
- **Plants** grow in clusters by habitat, with clearings and groves. Grass grows in clumps, with gusts running across
  far fields; plant stems are rooted and bark has a sheen.
- **Far views**: far trees and shrubs show as cheap stand-ins, so forests and shrubs stay visible from hundreds of
  metres away, and the far ground reaches about 3 km at High.
- Mesa arches take the local rock colour; tent hides look like leather.

### Water and weather
- **Water**: shore-facing swells that grow over the shallows, whitecaps, a surf line, a fine water sheet near you
  that dips under your feet, river falls that curl, whitewater on rapids, and molten lava.
- **Under water**: each water (sea, lake, swamp) has its own fog, with light shafts from above and quick surfacing.
  Sound is muffled under water, and walls and hills muffle sound above it.
- Caves have their own water, with waterfall curtains in the chutes.
- **Storms** have ragged, heaped clouds with matching shadows on the ground; rain is streaky and no longer greys out
  the ground nearby. Clouds look softer overall.
- The blue moon is a deep blue and lights the night blue; sunny days are less hazy.
- No more white circles or dots from bright lights, and no black shapes at roof eaves.

### Faster and steadier
- The island is saved after the first start and loaded on later starts, so starting is quicker.
- After a jump or a fresh start, big things (trees and buildings) load first, then smaller plants, and a short
  veil covers the first load and long jumps so you don't watch the world pop in.
- Cheaper sky and clouds, and sharper shadows from tall things like colossi and towers.
- Flickering flames and stray pink patches on some graphics cards fixed.

## Things to try
- Talk to a villager as a human, then as a rabbit and as a wolf.
- Put on a torch at night; take a delivery quest to another town.
- Walk the long tunnel under the border range end to end.
- Walk through a cave and a ruin, and open the chests.
- Climb a beacon tower in a big town at dusk.
- Dive in the sea, a lake and a swamp; stand behind a house and listen.
- Stand in a storm at noon and at night, and watch it from a hill.
- Look at a forest from a hill, and fly high over the island.
- Get close to a wolf mid-stride, and find a horse lying on a slope.

## Multiplayer
- **Builds**: everyone needs v0.14.0 (v0.13.0 players can't join a v0.14.0 game, or the other way round).
- Gear and talking to folk show for other players.

## Download
Unzip `Punchy-v0.14.0-windows.zip` anywhere and run `Punchy.exe`. Windows 64-bit only.
