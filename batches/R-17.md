**Why (Zach 11-12):** "The ocean, when seen from high up, still has significant tiling to its displacement. Would there be a
cheap way to add some low frequency noise that spans very long distances to break up the patterns?" Then: "Yes please".
Supervisor read of main: the wave groups are two crossed sines (GroupA 260 m, GroupB 410 m), so from altitude the big sets
and calm spells form a regular diamond lattice; the phase warp is two plain ~3 km sines; high up the short waves drop out
(PunchySwellWaveCount) and the long ones left all run in the sector's fixed headings, so crests line up in parallel.
