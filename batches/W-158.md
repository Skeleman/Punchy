**Why (Zach 11-14):** in broad daylight the beacon tower's stair doesn't stand out from the tower. The grounding
darkening (the darker bottom edge of a vertical face) should apply here as well: each step gets a darker base, and
the wall darkens where a step meets it. The same goes for every building: wherever faces meet, the lower face
darkens a little, standing in for dirt and ambient occlusion. Serves life and detail (2).

What exists today: Grounding counts height from the mesh's origin only, so a step high on a tower gets no base
shade. W-99's `Contacts` puts full-width horizontal bands on upright walls, columns and chimneys, and only where
floors, platforms, balconies or plinths meet them. Stairs, inside corners, eaves, porches and spiral runs get
nothing.
