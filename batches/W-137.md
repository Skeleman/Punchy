# W-137: cave mouths meet the ground exactly (world lane)

Status: open, NEXT after W-135 (ahead of W-136). Branch `world/mouth-rim`, new from origin/main.
Read first: COORDINATION.md (rules 15-21), TerrainSource.MouthCut / MeshCutDepth / MeshHeight, BuildChunkMeshJob.MouthCut

CaveSampling.SkinGap / MouthBlend, batches/W-112.md row 1 and the W-128 notes.

**Why (Zach 11-10, asked many times):** "Cave mouths still have a seam." W-112 and W-128 narrowed the gap but kept the
same method, which can't close it:
- the terrain isn't cut along a real edge. Each vertex gets a cut value and the pixel shader clips where the interpolated
  value goes negative, so the terrain's edge is a contour floating inside triangles;
- the cave walls are a separate mesh from the cave field, with a "skin" of ground 2 cm under the terrain;
- the two edges never share vertices, normals or texturing, so there is always a line: a small step, a crack, or a change
  in shading and grain where the terrain stops and the skin starts. Holes are cut only at terrain LOD <= 1.
