# P-26: talking to folk (player lane)

Branch `player/talk` from origin/main, worktree Punchy-player, after P-25 and after A-93 row 1 is merged (it gives
`FolkConversation.Topics/Ask` and `FolkPersona`; the answers and lines are the animal lane's). Zach 11-09: folk should feel
like people: ask who they are, ask favours, see how they're doing. Easy to template, changed later.

## Row 1: the talk window
- Use key (F/RB) on a folk in front of you (reuse P-25's forgiving prompt rules) starts a talk: the folk stops and faces
  you, the camera eases a little closer, a small window lists Topics(); pick one (mouse, keys 1-9, pad) and the answer
  shows with the speaker's name and job, the folk gestures. Esc/B or walking away ends it.
- Played as an animal: no menu, the folk's reaction plays (A-93 row 3) as a line over its head; Use again tries again.
- Works for a client in multiplayer (local only, nothing sent, like walk-by lines).

## Row 2: favours and the quest hook
- An answer with an offer: accept or decline in the window; accepted favours/quests go to the existing quest list
  (quests.json, P-24 owns tracking later), small gifts go to the inventory.
- A per-folk "you've met" memory for the session: they greet you by name the second time, and the first topic changes.

## Row 3: polish

- Shots: a talk as a human (3 topics), a favour accepted, the same folk met as an animal.
