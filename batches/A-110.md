Why (aim 2, Zach 11-13): a town's far figures must be its real people, in about the real number. Today a town
has household records for everyone (town 73: 101 members) but only its resident rows become folk (12), drawn anew
each visit, and the far figures are a separate set, so people vanish and others appear at the hand-over.

Model: every household member is one person with one identity (its Individual) and one day, `Whereabouts(member,
day, hour)`: a pure function of the household records and the shared clock, so every machine agrees with nothing
sent. A person has three forms: indoors (not drawn; the home sign shows them), far figure (drawn from Whereabouts),
and full creature (the host spawns that Individual at its Whereabouts spot when a player is in range).
