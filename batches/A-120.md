**Why (Zach 11-14):** folk should interact with the pet-sized animals. Some pets belong to households; people,
especially children, like to pick up what they can carry and take it inside. How much someone wants to pet animals
comes from their personality, and they do it now and then. Serves life (1) and detail (2).
