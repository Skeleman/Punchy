# A-93: folk personalities and what they answer; how folk treat an animal player (animal lane)

Branch `fauna/persona` from origin/main, worktree D:/Unity/Punchy, after A-92. Zach 11-09: folk should feel like people you
can talk to: ask who they are, ask favours, get a sense of how they're doing, all from character parameters. Easy to
template, changed later. Played as an animal, talk is shallow: they treat you as a pet, are confused you seem tame, or
are afraid of or repelled by you. The data and answers are the animal lane's (FolkTalk/DialogueArchive live here); the
talk window and player stats are the player lane's (P-26, P-27). Today: FolkTalkSystem (walk-by lines), DialogueArchive

## Row 1: personality, and the API P-26 builds on (land this first, merge early)
- `FolkPersona` per folk, seeded from its name/seed (no network: every machine derives the same): a handful of 0-1 traits,
  e.g. warmth, mood, trust, curiosity, courage, pride, and a "fear/love of beasts" trait; plus a current state from what
  the brain already knows (tired at night, busy at work, hungry, hurt, scared, weather). Keep the list in one place.
- `FolkConversation.Topics(folk, playerBody)` and `FolkConversation.Ask(folk, topic, playerBody) -> Answer` (text,
  gesture, and an optional offer such as a favour/quest id or "refuses"). Placeholder lines are fine in this row.
- Topics (broad, data-driven): who are you, how are you, what do you do, tell me about this town, any news/rumours,
  can you help me (ask a favour), do you need anything (offer help: the quest hook), goodbye.
- Tests: same seed same persona on two runs; every topic answers for every culture without errors.

## Row 2: the template and the lines
- dialogue.json gains `conversation`: per topic, lines keyed by trait bands (low/mid/high of one or two traits) and state,
  with the existing {slots} and culture fallback (species, base species, Any). A line can name a gesture and an offer.
- Favours: whether a folk helps (trust x warmth vs. how much you've asked lately and stance), what it gives (a small
  item, directions to a landmark, a rumour, a discount later) and a remembered "asked too often" cooldown.
- "How are you" reads the state honestly (tired, worried about wolves, happy after a festival...), so you can tell.
- Write enough lines that 20 folk in one town feel distinct: shot or text dump of 10 folk answering the same 3 topics.
- `_readme` in the json explains how to add a topic or a line, so Zach can edit it by hand.

## Row 3: an animal player
- Played as an animal, Topics() is short (or empty) and Ask() picks a reaction from persona x species x stance:
  treat it as a pet (call it, pat, offer food), puzzled that it's tame ("are you someone's?"), wary or scared (back away,
  shoo), repelled (rats, spiders, slimes...), awed (big or rare creatures). Each reaction has a gesture, a line and
  maybe a brain response (flee, approach, feed) using what the brain already does.
- Data too: `beastTalk` in dialogue.json, keyed by species groups and traits.
- Shots: the same folk met as a human, a rabbit, a wolf and a horse.
