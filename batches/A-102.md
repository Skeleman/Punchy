# A-102: diving and breath for everyone (animal lane, #37)

Tree: D:/Unity/Punchy-fauna, after A-100 (before A-101). Branch `fauna/breath`, new from origin/main. Pairs with P-34
(player lane: the breath meter on screen) and A-103 (folk lane: sea folk residents in the city). Read first: COORDINATION.md (rules 15-24),
CreatureBrainSystem.SensesMovement (Swim mode, SwimDepth, Swimmable), PlayerMovementSystem (swimming), FaunaDirector.SeaFolk.

**Why (Zach 11-12, #37):** "With them living underwater, there will need to be a means of NPCs swimming, and a breath meter.
This meter will function the way 'stamina' typically works in games. The underwater seafolk do not use up their breath
underwater. NPCs that do are forced to resurface."

Today folk and the player only swim at the surface. Nothing dives, and nothing has breath.

| Row | Do | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Breath, one model for every body.** A breath value (0-1) per creature and per player that works like stamina: it drains while the head is under water, refills quickly in air, and an empty meter forces the body up. Per species: a water-breather flag (fish, sea folk: never drains) and a hold time in seconds (people ~30 s, otters and seals longer, most land animals short). Creatures are server simulated; the player's own breath is local player state, sent with the avatar. Add "breath" to GLOSSARY.md. Solo, host, client. | `-breathtest`: a person and a cat held under water both surface when empty, a sea folk and a fish do not; values logged; fingerprint pin reported |
| 2 | **Folk dive.** Folk (and land animals that swim) can swim under the surface in 3D: a swim pose lying in the water, slow strokes, turning up and down. Breath decides: an air breather goes down only while its breath lasts and comes up to breathe (a short rest at the surface before it dives again). Sea folk (A-103's race) swim freely at any depth between the domes; coordinate the flag with A-103 through the supervisor. | Shots: a sea folk diving between the shell domes, a villager surfacing for air; NaN probe 0 |
| 3 | **The player dives.** In deep water the crouch button dives and jump rises (a stick-relative swim, like the climb). Breath drains under water; when empty, the player is pushed up and can't dive again until the meter has refilled a little. A sea folk player never drains. No damage yet. | Dive, surface and empty-meter clips, solo + client; P-34 can read the value |
