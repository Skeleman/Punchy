**Why (Zach, 11-09):** far shrubs (and later other things) need a cheap stand-in so their draw distance can grow. Flying is in the game, so
a flat camera-facing cutout breaks from above; octahedral impostors hold up from every altitude. Build it as one reusable path
(baker + shader + a DetailPolicy "impostor" level), not a shrub one-off.

| Row | Do | Done when |

Small frames to suit the low-res look (start 8x8 or 12x12 frames of ~64 px; make both configurable).
