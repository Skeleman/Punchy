# W-138: tunnels go through mountains (world lane)

Status: open, NOW in the release tree. Branch `world/mountain-tunnels`, new from origin/main.
Read first: COORDINATION.md (rules 15-21), RoadBaking.cs (W-110 tunnel block and the Tunnel* constants, the cost grid,
MaxGrade, Steep/reroute), CaveBaking.Tunnels.cs, TunnelPortalKit.cs, V3Ranges.cs, and "W-137 look-check" in
REPORTS-world.md (the 6 current portals, with the ground over each).

**Why (Zach 11-10):** today's tunnels bore through small walkable hills (3-14 m), so each arch stands in nearly flat
ground. "Tunnels should go through much larger hills, cliffs, or mountains, and be much longer as a result. It's pretty
senseless to build a tunnel through a tiny walkable hill. I envisioned the tunnels being a means of crossing the mountain
boundaries between biomes (not exclusively, though)."

W-137 row 2b's covered way (`world/mouth-rim` b5312063) is shelved: don't build on it.
