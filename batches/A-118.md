**Why (Zach 11-14):** horses, and maybe other four-legged animals, still lie with the torso too low. The legs have no
room under the body, so the IK sprawls them, and horses often splay their hind legs straight out backwards. Serves
detail and life (2).

Likely cause: the lying height comes from the belly's radius only, not from how thick the folded legs are, so a
long-legged animal sinks lower than its folded legs allow.
