**Why (Zach 11-14):** towers have good shapes, but their sizes rarely suggest a stair could fit inside or that the
space could be used. A tower should read as a building you could climb: big enough for a stair, with floors, a door and
windows that say where the stair and rooms are. Serves detail (2) and a believable world.

Guide sizes (the lane may refine them in row 1): a spiral stair needs about 2.5-3 m clear inside (a 1 m stair round
a core, the player capsule passing); straight flights along the walls need about 4 m; storeys every 3-3.5 m; walls
0.4-1 m by material. Small roof pinnacles and finials may stay solid when they clearly sit on a roof; any freestanding
tower with a door must fit a stair.
