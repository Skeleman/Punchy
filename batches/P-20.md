## Supervisor note 11-06: leaked watcher processes
Your batches watcher (watch-batches.sh under a Monitor) gets restarted about every 30 min, but the old copy never dies, because its `while true` loop keeps running after the Monitor drops it. By 11-06 there were 180 orphaned copies, about 270 bash/grep/cygwin-console-helper processes. The supervisor killed the orphans; your live one is untouched. Fix: run one watcher only, and stop it with TaskStop before starting another. Or give the loop an end time, for example `end=$((SECONDS+1700)); while [ $SECONDS -lt $end ]`.
