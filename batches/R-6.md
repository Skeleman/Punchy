**Why (Zach, 11-09):** big things are only special because they're seen from far away; they should follow the same size-vs-distance
rule as everything else, not one-off code. Today colossi have no LOD at all, three landmarks switch at fixed distances, and a flora
layer's fixed Radius can cut a model before its size reach says so. Kit buildings already do it right (whole-building LOD by the
size rule) but their far LOD still carries hidden insides.

| 0 | **One distance metric (do first).** FloraCull.compute mixes two: the reject and the cast test use 3D distance, but LOD level, the real-time shadow tier (`sharp`), shadow stillness and the draw fade (`keep`) use ground-plane distance (`d`, line ~120). From high ground the two disagree: trees pop instead of fading, and a tree can fall between the cascades (view depth) and the cache (ground distance) with no shadow.
