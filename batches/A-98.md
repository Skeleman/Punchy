# A-98: underwater sound, walls and openings

Lane: animal (owns `Scripts/Audio/**`). Tree: first free fauna tree after A-97 / A-91. Branch `fauna/underwater-sound`, new from origin/main.
Read first: COORDINATION.md (rules 15-20), GameAudio.cs, SoundSynth.cs, Camera/Underwater.cs (`Underwater.LensUnder`), WeatherSystem
weather audio.

**Why (Zach, 11-10, closes #22):** "We should add underwater sound. Just a sort of low-pass filter and pitch shift of other sounds with
an overlay of a stereotypical underwater hum background."

| Row | Do | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Muffle everything while the camera is under.** Drive from `Underwater.LensUnder` (camera, not player, so it matches the picture), eased ~0.25 s in/out. Every voice's low-pass drops to ~500-800 Hz and pitch to ~0.9; the weather/wind/rain beds too (rain becomes a soft patter on the surface or goes quiet). Volume of above-water sounds down a bit with depth. Use what GameAudio already has per voice (its air low-pass) rather than an AudioMixer asset; no ProjectSettings changes. Lava/swamp per fluid if `Underwater` knows the fluid (lava: no sound change needed if you can't swim in it). | Log lines with cutoff/pitch at surface, 2 m, 10 m; recorded 10 s clips above/under (wav to D:/Unity/PunchyMilestones/A-98/) |
| 2 | **Underwater hum bed.** A synthesised loop in SoundSynth: low rumble (~40-120 Hz filtered noise, slow swell), faint muffled bubbles and the odd creak/click. Louder with depth a little, fades in with the muffle. Bubbles when the player moves fast or surfaces. Its own level in SoundGroup.World. | Clips; no click at the loop point; voice count unchanged (bed outside the 24-voice pool or reserved) |
| 3 | **Edges.** Camera bobbing at the surface doesn't flutter (hysteresis); caves with water, the sea folk lake, menus/pause, client and host both (local only, nothing sent). Settings volume still applies. | PlayMode suite (rule 19); a short note of each edge checked |
| 4 | **Muffled by walls and hills (Zach 11-10: "yes").** Per playing voice, a line-of-sight check from listener to source against terrain and buildings (physics raycast or the existing cave-rock test, whichever is cheaper), staggered so only a few voices are tested per frame. Blocked: low-pass ~1-2 kHz and -6 to -10 dB, eased ~0.2 s so it never clicks. Thin things (fences, foliage) don't block. Reuse the per-voice air low-pass and the cave occlusion path; don't stack a second filter. | Clips: a folk talking behind a house, a wolf behind a hill, the same in the open. Cost: ms per frame for 24 voices (target <= 0.05 ms CPU) |
| 5 | **Sound goes round through openings.** When a voice is blocked, look for a short way round: through a building's open door or window, or a cave mouth, from the door/mouth points the world already has. If one is found, the voice sounds from that opening (pan/position), muffled by how far round it is. Indoors, outside sounds come in through the door. Cap the search (nearest few openings within ~30 m), cache per voice, refresh a few times a second. | Clips: standing inside a house with a folk outside the door, then round the corner; inside a cave hearing the mouth. Cost noted with row 4 |

Rows 4-5 are general (not only underwater); under water, row 1's muffle wins and rows 4-5 stack only if cheap and still clear.
