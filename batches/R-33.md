Technique: Voronoi plates (cells stretched about 2:1 where wanted), their borders found by distance-to-edge, the
border lines bent by a low-octave noise warp so they wander; each plate slightly domed (high in the middle, falling
toward its edge) and the crack itself a narrow, steep-walled fault. Height drives normal and darkening; no texture
art.
