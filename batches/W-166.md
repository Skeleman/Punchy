**Why (Zach 11-14):** built pieces look uneven depending on when they were made. Planks are the worst: some are flat
colour with toon edges, some are textured. He wants every kit piece to state what it is made of, so a look upgrade to
a material reaches every piece made of it. Kinds may differ (oak or pine, red or mud brick), but each family follows one
rule set: e.g. planks always use the wood grain, with base colours that vary by wood; brick always has normals with
light parallax unless it is real geometry. Serves detail (2) and a unified look.
