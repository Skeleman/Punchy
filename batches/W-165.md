The biome and surface assets have no owner: ask the supervisor before changing them.

**Why (Zach 11-14):** towns should have larger farm fields outside the town limits, not just small garden beds. Most
in temperate land, but other biomes can farm too, with invented crops that suit harsh places. Fields seen from a hill
are a big part of a landscape that feels lived in, so they serve the far view and life (1) and detail (2).

Starter crops (the lane may add more, all built procedurally from our own meshes and shaders):
- temperate: grain in rows that sway, cabbages, bean poles, an orchard row;
- cold and snow: snow potatoes (pale leafy mounds in banked rows, frost on the leaves), hardy kale, a covered cold frame;
- desert and volcanic: agave-like ferns (thick blue-grey rosettes) on terraces, with stone channels or ash mulch;
- marsh: rice-like paddies in shallow water (reuse W-153's paddy pools);
- salt flats: low salt-loving succulents in raised beds;
- elf towns: no wood (W-161): stone-edged beds with glowing herbs.
