**Why (Zach 11-14):** a lot of shadow flicker near the player. Everything cast on the stable while it was being shown

blades, so shaded ground under lit blades aliases strongly. Serves the far view (1) and detail (2).

Likely cause of the jiggle: the sun turns a little every frame, so the shadow map is re-projected every frame and
every edge crawls by part of a texel, even with the camera still. At night the same light is the dim moon, so the
crawl is there but too faint to see.
