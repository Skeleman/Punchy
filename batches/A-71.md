## Design

**Comfort profile per species and race:** a preferred range on each of heat, wetness (rain and snow falling on them), dryness/humidity, brightness and wind, with a tolerance. Seeded variation per individual, so a few are hardier or fussier than the rest. Discomfort builds over time out of range and fades in shelter. It doesn't flip on instantly.

Starting profiles (expand to every species and race):

| Who | Dislikes | Seeks |
|---|---|---|
| Bat folk, bats | brightness, sun | shadow, caves, tree shade, under roofs |
| Seafolk / fish folk | dry and hot (desert) | water, wells, rain, humid shade |
| Humans | rain, storms, deep cold | roofs, buildings, tree cover, fires |
| Turtle folk | heat | cold: water, shade, caves |
| Ogres | cold | fires, warm buildings, sun |
| Expand | e.g. pygmies dislike open sky in storms; cats hate rain; frogs and amphibians like rain; furry beasts tolerate cold but pant in heat; birds tuck in and roost in wind and rain; desert folk dislike cold nights | |

**Shelter sources** (each protects against some discomforts, with a radius):
- **Trees:** shade and shelter against brightness, heat and rain (less in a storm). Denser canopy protects more.
- **Fires and braziers:** warmth, and light (bad for bat folk).
- **Any water** (rivers, lakes, the sea, ponds, puddles, wells, fountains): humidity and cooling.
- **Roofs, buildings, awnings, tents, caves and overhangs:** rain, snow, sun and wind.
- **Rock shadow and cliff faces:** brightness and some wind.

Read these from existing world data (flora and settlement queries) without writing to world-owned files. If a source isn't queryable, add a read-only accessor in the animal tree, or list it in the hand-back for the world lane.

**Behaviour:** uncomfortable creatures look for the nearest suitable shelter within a search radius, weighted by how uncomfortable they are. They walk or flee there depending on severity, wait until comfortable, then go back to what they were doing. Crowding is OK, but space them out. If nothing is in reach, they show the discomfort and keep going: hunched, slower, panting, shivering, shading their eyes (bat folk already have an eye-shield pose), shaking off water. No pathfinding blow-ups: cap searches per frame.

**Lines (folk only):** a seeded chance to say something when discomfort crosses a threshold, when they reach shelter (relief), and occasionally while waiting. Pick lines by discomfort, race and demeanor. Rate-limit per speaker and per area so a crowd fleeing rain doesn't all talk at once. Animals don't talk; they get sounds and poses instead.

## Line bank

Write a data file (not hard-coded strings), keyed by discomfort x race x demeanor, with generic fallbacks. Recycle lines across races where they fit. At least 12 lines per discomfort in the generic pool, and at least 6 per discomfort for each race and demeanor that has its own voice, plus relief lines. Aim for 300+ lines in total. Match each race's existing voice from the dialogue system. Zach's seeds (keep them all):

- Too hot: "Hot, hot, hot!"
- Too hot or dry: "I think I'm going to faint!" / "What an arid torture!"
- Too cold: "My skin has turned to ice!" / "I must escape this frigid hellscape!"
- Escaping a storm: "I can't handle this!"

Discomforts to cover: too hot, too dry, too cold, rain, snow, storm and thunder, too bright, wind, and relief on reaching shelter. Demeanors: at least cheerful, grumpy, timid, proud and dramatic, plus whatever the dialogue system already uses. Examples of the range wanted: a grumpy ogre in snow ("Bah. Fire. Now."), timid bat folk in daylight ("Too bright... too bright..."), a proud seafolk in the desert ("This sand insults my gills."), relief ("Ahh, that's the stuff.").
