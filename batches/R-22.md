What main does today (supervisor's read, 8d1e6f1c; check each before relying on it):
- The island is about 9 km across. Ground is drawn to about 2 km at High (3 km with the far ring, High and Ultra only);
  Medium and Low have the plain disc. The island is never seen whole.
- Haze reaches 98% at the edge of the drawn terrain by definition, so it follows the draw distance, not an atmosphere.
  Ordinary days: about 62% haze at half range, 22% at a quarter. Clear sunny days use a cubic curve. No height term.
- Trees stop at the chunk disc. The far ring carries no flora and no forest stand-in: bare ground colour.
- Terrain LOD is by fixed distance bands (log2), the one system not on DetailPolicy.
