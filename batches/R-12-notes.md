# Punchy v0.12.0

## New since v0.11.0

### A new island
- **The island is remade**: five realms, mountains that keep their own look up their flanks, smooth winding rivers
  that widen as they go, and waterfalls wherever a river drops. Every place has moved, so explore it fresh.
- **Grand falls** pour from the mountains into the central lake.
- An **archipelago** of small islands lies off the dune coast.
- **Icebergs** float off the cold coasts, and one of them has a cave you can walk into.
- Fewer but bigger ponds; swamp pools step down hillsides like paddy fields.
- A climbable **salt tower** of basalt stands on the largest salt flat.
- A **sunken temple** lies half buried in the dunes, and you can go inside.

### Menu and settings
- **Esc** (or **Start** on a controller) opens a new main menu with Multiplayer, Graphics and Character tabs. It works
  fully with a controller and shows the right button icons.
- **Character tab**: pick your body by kind (humanoid, four-legged, bird, fish...), then the species, then its colours.
  Other players see your choice.
- Resolution and window mode are in the Graphics tab.
- Typing on a controller: a text wheel lets you enter a name or join code.
- An **Exit game** button sits at the foot of the menu.

### Playing as animals
- Four-legged animals gallop and bound for real; wolves, dogs, cats and coyotes have a leaping run.
- The right face button rolls on the ground, and dives down when flying or swimming. Fish swim faster when sprinting
  and hold their depth.
- Climbing: the stick moves you along the surface you're on, and wall climbers can grip the walls of buildings.
- Caves: slopes are walkable, you no longer slip into the rock, and the camera stays out of the walls.
- Cats and dogs have more voices, and their repeated calls sound different each time.

### Creatures and folk
- **Villagers have jobs**: woodcutters, smiths, fishers at river crossings and more, with work poses, tools and
  lines to match. They stop and chat with each other now and then, with a speech bubble you can see.
- Folk react to the body you play: humans, dwarves and elves each have their likes and dislikes.
- Chases close in and can end in a catch; dogs hunt hares and growl at them.
- Each animal has its own temper: some are bolder, some run sooner.
- A **carnivorous plant** grows in marshes, swamps and fungal forests and snaps at anything that comes close.
- In the desert, an ocotillo is not always a plant: some get up and scuttle away on their arms.
- A **skunk** sprays a cloud of gas as it flees.
- Songbirds take off and land smoothly, and birds turn more naturally in the air.
- Animals keep out of cave walls, towers and icebergs.

### Settlements
- **Fishing villages** at river mouths, with docks, pier networks and houses on stilts.
- **Sea folk** live on the bed of the largest deep lake, with a pier you can walk onto from the bank.
- **Turtle folk** settle on the highest mountaintops, in snowy igloo-like domes.
- **Bat folk** hang their houses on cave walls and ceilings above white pools.
- Kit-built cities and ruins, with mossy stone and torch-lit rooms.
- Huge **stone colossi** lie across the land, half buried.
- Hermits, campsites and great mushrooms take on the look of their biome; harsh lands have shelters.
- Snow settles on village props in cold places; houses have chimney smoke, bevelled eaves and banners that flutter.
- Bridges end their rails on posts; cobblestones are rounded and uneven.

### Sky
- New **sunrise and sunset** colours across the sky, clouds and sunlight.
- The **blue moon** is much larger, rocky, with a single crack and slow drifting tendrils.
- Shooting stars are rarer and fade out gently; a few burn red-orange.

## Multiplayer
- **Joining no longer drops you while your island builds.** Joining players get much longer to build the world before
  the game gives up on them.
- **The host no longer turns away its own game.** Hosting a game on the new island works again from the start.
- If a player built a different world, the host tells them and disconnects them, instead of letting them play in
  a mismatched world.
- **Builds**: everyone needs v0.12.0 (the world changed, so v0.11.0 and v0.12.0 players can't join each other).
- **Tested** by hosting and joining on one PC.

## Known issues
- The game can crash if you host a second time in one session (Host, stop, Host again). Restart the game before hosting again. Fix on the way (#99).

## Download
Unzip `Punchy-v0.12.0-windows.zip` anywhere and run `Punchy.exe`. Windows 64-bit only.
