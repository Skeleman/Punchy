**Why (Zach 11-10):** on a controller, up/down in the character selection menu steps one item by index, the same as left/right.
"It would be great for the controller directions to map to the UI layout": up/down should move by row, left/right within a row.

Today MenuNav keeps the stops as a flat list in draw order: up/down changes Focus by +/-1 and left/right on a non-slider does the
same, so a grid of swatches or species walks one cell at a time in both axes.
