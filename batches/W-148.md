pygmy caves should be considered water. Water should no longer be rendered as disks. This includes the water in the
fountain in elven towns."

pieces (its mesh layout, shore, flow, the water field and patch near the player, swimming and wading), shaped to its basin,
never a round disc laid over it. Lava is not water: leave lava/toxic glow pools as they are unless the fix covers them free.
