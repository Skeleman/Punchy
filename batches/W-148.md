# W-148: all water is real water, no discs (world lane, #277)

Tree: Punchy-world. Branch `world/no-water-discs` from origin/main. Read first: CaveSystem.Water.cs (W-134 baked cave water,
WhitePools), CaveSystem.cs (landmark pool discs ~line 856, white pool draws, _SoftRim), BatFolkBaking.cs (WhitePools),
FloraSystem.cs (~288-320, fountain water discs), FloraGenerator.Town.cs (~336, the fountain basin stand-in disc),
BuildChunkMeshJob.cs (~181, landmark pools left out of the sheet), DungeonProps.cs (~235 well), CaveBaking.cs (~1190).

**Why (Zach 11-12):** "the lakes inside of caves should be built out of normal water pieces. The white water in batfolk/
pygmy caves should be considered water. Water should no longer be rendered as disks. This includes the water in the
fountain in elven towns."
So every body of water, in caves, landmarks, towns and dungeons, is the same water as the surface: the terrain water's
pieces (its mesh layout, shore, flow, the water field and patch near the player, swimming and wading), shaped to its basin,
never a round disc laid over it.

**Zach 11-12 (later):** "Lava and toxic pools should become real liquid. In geysers, the effect used when an entity moves
through water should be repeatedly stimulated in the center, subtly, to help sell the look of water interaction in the center."
So lava and toxic pools are the same basin-fitted liquid pieces with their own look (lava: slow, glowing, no swimming,
the existing burn/harm rules; toxic: its own colour and harm). And a geyser's pool gets a gentle repeating disturbance at
its vent from the water field (WaterPatchSystem sources), as if something small kept stirring it.
